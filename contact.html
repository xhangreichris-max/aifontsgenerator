What You Can Write About

  • Bug reports: a copy button that does nothing, a page that won't load, or a tool that freezes.
  • A style that renders wrong: letters that turn into boxes, question marks or the wrong characters on your phone or computer.
  • Feature ideas: a font style, a platform guide or a small tool you would like to see here.
  • Press: interviews, reviews or questions about how the generator works.
  • Partnerships: embedding the generator on your site, using the API, or working together on a project.

Reporting a Bug

A few details help us find and fix a problem much faster. Please include:

  • your device and operating system, for example an Android phone or a Windows laptop
  • the app or browser where it went wrong, such as Instagram, Discord or Chrome
  • the exact text you typed and the name of the style you picked
  • a screenshot, if you can take one
