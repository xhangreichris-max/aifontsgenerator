What is gothic/blackletter text and where does it come from?

Gothic or blackletter text uses characters from the Mathematical Fraktur Unicode block. The shapes descend from the dense lettering of medieval European scribes and early printed books. A generator simply maps ordinary letters onto those Unicode points so the historical look appears in modern apps.
