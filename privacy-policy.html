Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The Short Version

AI Fonts Generator (aifontsgenerator.com) is run by Chrismark Studio. We don't ask you to sign up, and we don't collect your name, email address or password. The site does use cookies for two things: Google Analytics, which tells us how the site is used, and Google AdSense, which shows the ads that keep the tools free. This page explains both and how to opt out.

Your Text Stays in Your Browser

Nothing you type into a generator is sent to our servers. The font conversion runs entirely in your browser. We do not log, store or transmit your input text in any form.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and to other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites. These cookies may record which pages you viewed and which ads you saw or clicked, so that the ads you see are more relevant and the same ad isn't repeated too often.

Other ad vendors or ad networks may also serve ads on this site and may use their own cookies for the same purpose.

How to Opt Out of Personalised Ads

You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads). If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they won't be based on your browsing history.

You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site: which pages are visited, how long people stay, and what kind of device and browser they use. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this. We only see this data in aggregate — totals and trends — and we don't use it to identify individual visitors. It helps us decide which tools to improve and which styles to add next.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Every font generator on this site still works with cookies turned off. Blocking cookies may mean the ads you see are less relevant, but it won't stop the tools from working.

Google's Privacy Policy

Google Analytics and Google AdSense are operated by Google and are covered by Google's own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how the site uses cookies or data, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top.
